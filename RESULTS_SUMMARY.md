## Other findings

* Within IDH-wildtype, IDH-mutant and glioblastoma strata (secondary), AUROC of all models was 0.568 to 0.707.
* Class weighting shifted network calibration intercepts (for example −1.54 at 12 months);
  without weighting, intercepts of the best networks were close to zero; AUROC changed by at most 0.019.
* Gravendeel (set B): all models underestimated risk (penalised LR intercept 1.07, compact MLP 0.42);
  after intercept updating the Brier scores were 0.164 and 0.161.
* Original time-to-event analysis (full follow-up, Cox, fixed penalties): Harrell's C 0.853 in TCGA (set B),
  0.725 in Gravendeel and
  0.620 in REMBRANDT (set A).

## Post hoc analyses (protocol version 1.2)

Defined after the results of the planned analyses were known; exploratory.

* R1, selection repeated in every bootstrap resample (primary setting): −0.004 to 0.010.
* R2, compact MLP versus penalised LR without selection: 90% interval −0.003 to 0.008 (within +/-0.03 in 10 of 18 settings);
  per-repeat difference −0.002 (SD 0.005).
* R3, class weighting: weighted calibration intercepts track the logit of the training event rate (r = 0.99, compact network);
  12-month Brier score 0.132 with and 0.093 without weighting; AUROC changed by at most 0.019.

  Cox-loss MLP minus penalised Cox 0.000 (−0.008, 0.007).
* R5, nested tuning of both model classes: tuned MLP minus tuned penalised LR 0.000 (−0.008, 0.008).
* R6, network initialisation on fixed folds: difference from penalised LR −0.025 to −0.001.
* R7, Gravendeel set B with recorded IDH status: compact MLP minus penalised LR +0.008 (−0.012, 0.027).
